**Knowledge management (KM)** is the **process** side: <br>
how knowledge gets _created, captured, stored, shared, and applied_ so it actually changes what someone can do (instead of rotting in an archive like most corporate initiatives). A widely used process view treats organizations as “knowledge systems” that **generate, codify, store, distribute, and apply** knowledge.

A **knowledge management system (KMS)** is the tooling/infrastructure that supports those processes (repositories, search, collaboration spaces, etc.). <br>
>*reference*:<br>["Knowledge management and knowledge management systems: conceptual foundations and an agenda for research"](https://flora.insead.edu/fichiersti_wp/inseadwp1998/98-57.pdf)
