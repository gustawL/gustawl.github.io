A **knowledge** **base** is a curated collection of short, <br>focused entries that answer specific questions or document procedures; <br>
it’s designed for quick retrieval and reuse. <br><br>
**Knowledge** **management** <br>is the broader discipline that covers the creation, <br>capture, organization, sharing, and application of knowledge to improve learning and decision‑making.<br>
<br>
**Personal** **knowledge** **management** (PKM) <br>applies those same ideas at the individual level: capture what you learn, organize it so you can find it, and reuse it to create new work.
