Luhmann’s Zettelkasten was (i think) an early version of what we might now call a “second brain.” He describes how a well-structured external system can become more than memory or storage - a "thinking partner". <br>His "second memory (Zweitgedächtnis)", which we might nickname a ‘second brain’, wasn’t about tasks and goals, but about **insight and intellectual dialogue**.
