Warning! "hardened" privacy browsers have many default functions disabled. Expect that something will not work here "magically" by default. <br>
Back in the day, people spent days to configure tweaking browser for privacy `about:config`. 
## useful when...
- **You don’t want advertisers following you around**:
  Many sites try to track what you read, watch, or buy. Privacy browsers block those trackers. Ads were, and always will be annoying.
