Luhmann’s Zettelkasten was (I think) an early version of a “[[20251228015540|second brain]]”  
He describes how an external system can become more than memory or storage: it can be a “thinking partner” [^1].  <br>
His “second memory (Zweitgedächtnis)”—what we might call a second brain - wasn’t about tasks and goals, but about insight and intellectual dialogue.
