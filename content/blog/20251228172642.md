I’m responsible for my [[projects/digital garden/index|project]]. I’m also a tester. <br><br>
Keeping the website live, with periodic reviews and small observations, helps me step into the role of the audience, analyzing it and improving the site. Working in public under my own name - creates useful pressure and momentum.<br><br>
Since this site is not only a blog but also a evolving portfolio, I prefer to have a captured record of how I think and how I handle challenges or plan ahead.<br><br>
The fact is, that’s what it’s meant for: <br>
documenting skills and showcasing them, sharing knowledge with others, improving myself as a person, and gaining cognitive benefits, etc. i do this not only for reuse of my knowledge.<br> <br><br>
Actually, I’m working on foundations.<br> I don’t want to regret anything later when it comes to the structure of this website. I need a friction‑free workflow as the final result of my current efforts.
