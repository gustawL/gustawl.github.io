Sharing is a form of love when what we give is not later used as leverage.

If, after the fact, someone presents an invoice (“look how much I’ve done for you”), the act stops being a gift and becomes an investment with expected returns - or a tool of pressure.

> [!note] Bats and altruism.  
> [Reciprocal food sharing in the vampire bat | Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/308181a0)
> Food sharing depends on kinship *and* reciprocity; unrelated bats also reciprocate in controlled settings.
