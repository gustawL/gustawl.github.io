I’m responsible for my [[projects/digital garden/index|project]]. I’m also a tester. <br><br>
Keeping it live, along with occasional reviews and observation, helps me step into the role of the audience. Since this site is not only a blog but also a portfolio, I prefer to have a record of how I think and how I handle challenges or plan ahead.<br><br>
The fact is, that’s what it’s meant for: documenting skills and showcasing them, sharing knowledge with others, improving myself as a person, and gaining cognitive benefits, etc. <br><br>
Actually, I’m working on foundations.<br> I don’t want to regret anything later when it comes to the structure of this website. I need a friction‑free workflow as the final result of my current efforts.
It’s a deliberate decision. I'm aware that everything takes time — no rush.
