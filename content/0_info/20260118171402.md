I’m publishing a roadmap for this project as part of my practice (human-led, AI-assisted).

You might wonder why I’m listing my ideas publicly, or ask the critical question: “Is that wise?” Personally, I don’t worry about competition. Nor do I fear that someone will “steal my ideas” and build/make them faster.

I’m not afraid because I know that everyone (including me and you) has their own unique style, character… a mark. Knowing that, I don’t worry much about it.

I am one of a kind - just like everyone else. "==No one is perfect, and that’s perfect==". [^1]

> [!tip] When you don't know what to do, pick and do whatever from the list. 

> [!tip] The literature list is now ordered from highest to lowest priority (1 = highest).

> [!note] Every record here have purpose, or destination.

> [!warning] Don't worry. No rush. You don't need to publish everyday (but do something in that direction - learning, online studies, reading, practicing critical thinking on ai) ... learn your craft. 
## Literature
I’m aiming for public-domain sources and studying the pioneers. That simplifies the creative process and reduces copyright-related complexities. However, I treat citation and source attribution as a non-negotiable quality standard, not an option. I can develop in a smart way.

> [!reminder] Don't just read, slow down.
