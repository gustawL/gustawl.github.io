## Scope
These rules apply to this website, the comments section, and any associated social spaces (current or future). **These rules apply to the owner and moderators as well.**  
Moderation may include removal, locking, muting, blocking, or banning. Enforcement is at moderator discretion.

Current moderators: Only me (Gustaw)
## No Politics or Ideological Advocacy
Content that promotes political parties or candidates, advocates for political causes, or uses rhetoric/symbols strongly associated with political movements will be removed. This includes (but isn’t limited to) posts, comments, avatars, usernames, symbols, slogans, hashtags, and indirect signaling.

This is not a place for political debate, campaigning, or ideological recruitment. Removals under this rule are not up for discussion.

**Exceptions:**
- The Free Software Movement
- Information Freedom
- Open Source Software
- Other broadly pro-social or educational movements **only when directly relevant to the topic and not used for recruitment, campaigning, or signaling**

## No advertisements
Advertising and promotional content are not allowed.
Links must be directly relevant; link shorteners and tracking parameters may be removed.

**What counts as advertising:**
- unsolicited promotions (“check out my product/service”), recruitment pitches
- affiliate/referral links, discount codes, giveaways, airdrops
- repetitive link-dropping, SEO-style comments, “DM me” funnels
- sponsored content or attempts to buy placement/mentions

**Exceptions:**
- a brief, relevant introduction of who someone is and what they do (role/expertise/portfolio link) **only when directly relevant to the discussion**
- referencing tools, companies, or resources **when needed for context or learning** (e.g., PowerShell, Microsoft, iFixit). Naming something for clarity is not advertising.

This space is not a marketing channel. References or quotes may appear for informational context only. Promotional content may be removed.
