The following applies to this website, the comments section, and all of my social groups (current and future ones). **These rules apply to me as well**.

## No Politics or Ideological Advocacy
Any content promoting political parties, referencing political matters, or using language characteristic of specific political movements will be removed without discussion. This includes posts, comments, symbols, slogans, or indirect signaling.

Exceptions (limited and clearly defined):  
The Free Software Movement, Information Freedom, and Open Source Software.
