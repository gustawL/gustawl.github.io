## How I Use AI on This Blog

This blog uses AI tools such as:<br>
**OpenAI’s GPT** (and OpenAI image-generation models such as **DALL·E**)
and **Copilot** to assist with:

- "Wording work" (proposing alternative words, sentences, paragraphs) and grammar suggestions. 
- Research and web browsing to locate sources (then verified by me).
- Generating images (profile pictures, illustrations, site assets), edited later.

> ⚠️ **No content here is published automatically.** All material generated with AI is **reviewed, edited, and approved by me personally** before it appears online[^8].  
> When AI helps locate sources, I verify important claims against primary sources (papers, standards, official documentation) before publishing.

### Legal Compliance

The **EU Artificial Intelligence Act** (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) entered into force on **1 August 2024** and applies in stages:
- **2 February 2025** (selected chapters),
- **2 August 2025** (additional provisions),
- **2 August 2026** (general application),
- **2 August 2027** (certain obligations, including those linked to Article 6(1))[^2].

Under this regulation, transparency duties may apply depending on context (Article 50)[^3]. In particular:

1. If an AI system generates or manipulates **image/audio/video deepfake content**, deployers must disclose that it was artificially generated or manipulated (with limited exceptions)[^3].
2. If an AI system generates or manipulates **text published to inform the public on matters of public interest**, deployers must disclose that it was artificially generated or manipulated, **unless** the content has undergone **human review/editorial control** and a person holds **editorial responsibility** for publication (Recital 134)[^3][^4].

✅ My practice follows these principles:
- All AI-assisted content on this blog is **reviewed, edited, and curated by me**.  
- I maintain a **public audit trail** of changes and review via Git commits.

Where disclosure is required, I disclose. Where the law recognizes an exception for **human editorial control** (Recital 134), my workflow ensures I take responsibility for what is published. I do not publish raw AI output.

### Public Proof of Human Oversight

Every article, image, or code snippet that involved AI assistance is **reviewed and committed publicly through GitHub**. Where available, commits are **signed (SSH/GPG/S/MIME) or GitHub-verified**, which provides cryptographic confidence about the origin of a change[^7]. Each commit includes:

- a timestamp,  
- a human-written commit message (some are informal like "update content", but I aim to improve this),  
- a visible diff of changes.

📁 **View my GitHub commit history (public audit trail / evidence of editorial review):**  
👉 https://github.com/gustawl/gustawl.github.io/commits

This public log acts as a **verifiable audit trail**, meeting current best practices for responsible AI use[^5][^6]: **AI suggests, I decide.**

## Why I Use AI

The job market is evolving, and I want to adapt to its new demands. Today, different skill sets are valued.

I believe that in the near future, understanding and working with AI will be an essential professional skill.  
As remote work becomes an attractive option and local opportunities remain limited, I’m investing in learning how to collaborate with AI productively — not as a shortcut, but as a tool for modern, responsible work.

## For Visitors

I understand that AI tools must be used responsibly and not spread false or misleading information. That’s why I take care to review, verify, and edit all AI-assisted content before publishing.

Still, I’m only human — mistakes can happen. If you notice any incorrect, misleading, or outdated information on my website, I warmly invite you to let me know via email.

We are all responsible for the quality of content on the internet. I appreciate any effort to help improve the accuracy of this blog, and I will gladly review and correct any legitimate concerns.
