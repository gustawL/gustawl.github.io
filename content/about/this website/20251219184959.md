## How I Use AI on This Website

### Tools

I use:
- OpenAI text models (GPT) and OpenAI image-generation models (for example, DALL·E)
- MS Copilot (i subscribe MS office)

These tools assist me with:
- Wording and grammar (alternative sentences, paragraphs, tone, clarity).
- Research support (finding candidate sources and references).
- Generating images (illustrations, avatars, and site assets), which I later edit.
- Study.

>[!Caution] No automatic publishing
>Nothing on this site is published automatically. Any AI-assisted material is **reviewed, edited, and approved by me** before it appears online. 

### My practice

- All AI-assisted content on this website is **reviewed, edited, and curated by me**.
- I maintain a public change history through Git commits (a visible diff and timestamps).
- I do not publish deceptive impersonations or “deepfake” media.
- This site does not run autonomous publishing agents. I use off-the-shelf tools (listed at the beginning) for drafting and assistance; final publication and reviews are manual.
- I disclose AI assistance where required, and I aim to disclose it consistently as a matter of reader trust.

### Legal and transparency notes (EU)

The EU Artificial Intelligence Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) entered into force on **1 August 2024** and applies in stages:
- **2 February 2025**: prohibited AI practices and AI literacy obligations apply
- **2 August 2025**: governance rules and obligations for general-purpose AI (GPAI) models apply
- **2 August 2026**: the AI Act becomes fully applicable (general application)
- **2 August 2027**: extended transition for certain high-risk AI systems embedded in regulated products

Under Article 50, transparency duties can apply depending on context:
1. If an AI system generates or manipulates **image/audio/video** content constituting a *deepfake*, deployers must disclose that the content was artificially generated or manipulated (with limited exceptions).
2. If an AI system generates or manipulates **text** published to inform the public on matters of public interest, deployers must disclose that the text was artificially generated or manipulated, **unless** it underwent **human review/editorial control** and a natural or legal person holds **editorial responsibility** for publication.

My workflow and website is designed around constant **human editorial control** and review: AI may suggest; I decide; I publish. I do not publish raw AI output.

## What It Means for You, the Reader

- Content on this website is curated and approved by a real person (me).
- I use AI tools for assistance, but the final output reflects **human review, judgment, and intent**. Review is ongoing; this [[about/this website/index|website]] is designed for revisiting and improving content over time.
- I update this notice when laws, tools, or my workflow change.

I aim to use AI responsibly and avoid spreading false or misleading information. Even so, mistakes can happen. If you notice incorrect, misleading, or outdated information, contact me by email and I will review legitimate concerns and correct them where appropriate.<br>
>[!caution] Important take
>When I use AI, I do **not** enter personal information about other people.
>If I process documents like a CV, any real identifiers (names, company names, email addresses, phone numbers, locations, IDs) are **always replaced with placeholders** before I share the text.
>
> Examples: `PERSON_1`, `COMPANY_A`, `EMAIL_X`, `CITY_Y`.
